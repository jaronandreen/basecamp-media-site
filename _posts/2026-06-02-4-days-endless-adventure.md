With Spring season at its peak, we HAD to take advantage of a beautiful weekend when our work/life schedules allowed it.

Jaron was able to get a rare 4-day weekend and our dog-sitter's availability lined up perfectly to allow us to load up the gear trailer and camper van and head out on an hour and half drive to Reliance, TN to attempt to fit all of our hobbies into these 4 days.

Friday: 30-mi gravel bike ride.\
Saturday: Paddleboarding the Hiwassee.\
Sunday: First proper Dual-sport motorcycle ride.\
Monday: Fishing the Hiwassee River and surrounding creeks.
